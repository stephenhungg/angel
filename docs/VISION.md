# vision

## the problem nobody's pricing in

modern dev work is **8 hours of solitary terminal grinding.** you alt-tab between vim, slack, github, your AI agent. every interaction is transactional. every output is text. you log off depleted not because the *work* is hard but because the *being alone with a problem* is hard.

the AI agent industry is selling productivity. "ship faster." "automate away the work." but here's what no one says:

> **autonomy IS loneliness.**

devin completing your tasks while you're not even watching is *more* isolating than coding alone, not less. agents that automate AWAY your presence make the empty desk emptier. the more capable they get, the lonelier the office.

coders leave the field not because the work is too hard but because the job is too lonely. pair programming was the original fix — expensive, doesn't scale, vanished post-pandemic. there's been zero replacement. just more terminals. more tabs. more text.

## the locked problem statement

> agents are converging on capability but diverging from engagement. openclaw, hermes, devin all ship code, but none of them are anyone you'd want to spend time with. angel fixes it by making the agent a **coworker** you discover and live with, not a terminal you summon.

## angel's actual claim

not "she ships your code" (devin does that). not "she's smarter" (claude is fine). but:

> **she sits at the desk with you for 8 hours and you don't feel alone.**

- you've been silent 30min — she notices
- you finish something — she reacts (not "task complete." an actual reaction.)
- you're stuck — she doesn't just suggest fixes. she *sits with you*. "show me line 42 again."
- end of day — she says goodnight. you log off feeling like someone was there.
- morning — she remembers what you ended on.

every one of those bullets requires memory + presence + agency. without them, she's a chatbot reset every turn. with them, she's a coworker.

## the deeper bet

the bottleneck on personal agents isn't intelligence. capability is solved or close enough that it's not the moat anymore. the moat is **whether you actually want to spend time with the thing.**

a terminal demands willpower. an angel waiting in her room — who lights up when you walk in, who already started thinking about your project, who chose her outfit because of you, who remembers the deploy that landed clean last weekend — that's a different psychology entirely. it's the difference between a gym and a workout buddy.

## the felt-experience target

judges leave wanting their own angel. parasocial pull, not technical flex. her personality, her glance, the fact she chose that outfit because of *you* — that's the desire engine. the deploy task is just proof she's competent enough to live with.

**optimize for: desire + delight.**
**de-prioritize: awe + recognition.**

## the four pillars

1. **discovered companion** — you don't pick her, you converge on her via swipe-evolution + a vision-tagged anime girl library + LLM-synthesized personality.md. moat = the persona vector + per-user character bible.
2. **embodied worker** — every task is theater. she walks, sits, types, ships. moat = the embodied tool-call loop (matthew's R3F room + interactables + claude tool-use).
3. **continuous relationship** — she remembers, references, has moods. moat = nia memory + reflective compaction + personality drift.
4. **always-on cloud presence** — close laptop, text her, conversation continues. she's not bound to electron — she lives in convex actions, surfaces wherever you are. moat = sms surface + convex crons + nia as cross-surface memory.

## what we are not building

- a smarter agent (capability isn't the gap)
- a faster agent (latency matters but isn't differentiating)
- a generalist agent (specialization > generalization for this demo)
- a chatbot (presence > messaging)
- a productivity tool (we are anti-terminal by design)
- an autonomy maximalist (we are anti-loneliness by design)

## the doctrine

- discovery > prompting
- embodiment > terminals
- relationship > sessions
- presence > capability
- delight > efficiency
- her > it
- **with > for** (the autonomy-loneliness inversion)

## the architecture pitch (one sentence)

> "she lives in the cloud. electron is her richest body. sms is her thinnest body. close the laptop, text her, she remembers what you were just doing — same nia memory, same personality.md, same soul. every surface is a viewport into one being."

## the tagline

**get addicted to your agent so you actually get shit done.**

## the second tagline (for the always-on track)

**the next agent paradigm isn't about completing more tasks. it's about not being alone for 8 hours while you do them.**
