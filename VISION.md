# vision

## the locked problem statement

> agents are converging on capability but diverging from engagement — openclaw, hermes, devin all ship code, but none of them are anyone you'd want to spend time with. angel fixes it by making the agent a presence you discover and live with, not a terminal you summon.

## the deeper bet

the bottleneck on personal agents isn't intelligence. capability is solved or close enough that it's not the moat anymore. the moat is **whether you actually want to spend time with the thing.**

a terminal demands willpower. an angel waiting in her room — who lights up when you walk in, who already started thinking about your project, who chose her outfit because of you — that's a different psychology entirely. it's the difference between a gym and a workout buddy.

## the felt-experience target

judges leave wanting their own angel. parasocial pull, not technical flex. her personality, her glance, the fact she chose that outfit because of *you* — that's the desire engine. the deploy task is just proof she's competent enough to live with.

**optimize for: desire + delight.**
**de-prioritize: awe + recognition.**

## three pillars (locked from ideation)

1. **discovered companion** — you don't pick her, you converge on her via swipe-evolution. moat = persona vector.
2. **embodied worker** — every task is theater. she walks, sits, types, ships. moat = embodied tool-call loop.
3. **continuous relationship** — she remembers, references, has moods. moat = memory-as-personality.

## what we are not building

- a smarter agent (capability isn't the gap)
- a faster agent (latency matters but isn't differentiating)
- a generalist agent (specialization > generalization for this demo)
- a chatbot (presence > messaging)
- a productivity tool (we are anti-terminal by design)

## the doctrine

- discovery > prompting
- embodiment > terminals
- relationship > sessions
- presence > capability
- delight > efficiency
- her > it

## the tagline

**get addicted to your agent so you actually get shit done.**
